# Post-review checks against the professor's comments (21 Oct 2026)

On 21 Oct 2026 we audited the project against every comment the professor wrote on the 56 group proposals. Sheet A covers groups A1–A29 and sheet B covers B1–B26. Our group is B19. The groups using the same data are A11, A14, A20, B10, B22, B24 and B26. The audit produced 76 findings, and a verifier re-checked each one; 12 were confirmed fine. This file lists:

- the other 64 findings (status *partial*, *missing* or *bug* after verification);
- the 26 points the verifiers said the auditors had missed.

For each point it gives what we found, what we changed and what the report and slides still need. Exact duplicates share a row, and nothing is left out. Group ids in brackets are the proposals whose comments raise the point.

> **Ground rules held by every check below**
>
> - **Training rows only.** Every new analysis uses the 20,975 training rows and the five shared folds (`common.CV`, StratifiedKFold 5, shuffle, seed 2607526; asserted equal to `data/processed/cv_folds.csv`). No new script does any of the following:
>   - opens `data/processed/test.csv`;
>   - calls `load_split()` or `evaluate()`;
>   - reads a `*_p_test.npy` file.
>
>   A grep of the six new scripts for "test" finds only docstrings, the `n_test` argument of the Nadeau–Bengio correction and GridSearchCV's inner `mean_test_score` keys. Three of the scripts were also re-run under a file-open audit hook; none opened the test file.
> - **No frozen test result changed.** The 46 entries in the `models` block of `results/frozen_manifest.json` are identical to the last commit. No `*_p_test.npy`, `results/<slug>.json` or `model_comparison.csv` file was modified. `freeze.py --check` prints "OK: every frozen configuration matches its record".
> - **Post-freeze sensitivity checks only.** The new numbers select nothing. No new configuration was frozen, and none was scored on the test set. Any sentence that quotes them must say "training 5-fold CV, not scored on test".
> - **Paired comparisons.** These give per-fold values (in the CSVs), the mean difference, the number of folds where it is positive (written k/5) and a Nadeau–Bengio corrected 95% interval with n_test/n_train = 1/4 (written NB [lo, hi]).
> - **Appendix tables.** These are generated by `code/make_report_appendix.py`. Edit the generator, never `report/sec_appendix.tex` by hand. None of `report/` or `slides/` has been edited by these checks: every "still to change" item below is a report revision pending.

## 10. R cross-check (lab-notes style; training folds only)

`code/r_crosscheck/r_crosscheck.Rmd` refits the course's R models on the same five training folds (from `data/processed/cv_folds.csv`), and `results/r_crosscheck.csv` compares them with Python.

- **Agreement.** 13 of the 15 R fits that mirror a Python model are within 0.005 CV AUC, and the two rankings correlate at 0.99 (Spearman). glm reproduces the plain logit exactly (0.76024), which also confirms that the R logit reads PAY as a number, like the frozen model. The other close fits are MASS::lda (0.75721), class::knn K = 101 (0.77001 against 0.77003), e1071::svm (0.76353 against 0.76354) and xgboost (0.78619 against 0.78629).
- **QDA.** MASS::qda (0.7394, against 0.7512) has no regularisation, so it reproduces the frozen script's unregularised grid point. A hand-written QDA with r = 0.5 matches the frozen model exactly.
- **randomForest.** It scores 0.7743 against 0.7849 because it uses hard votes and lets leaves shrink below 20 rows. With soft votes and nodesize 80 it comes within 0.002 (0.7828).
- **Lab-default tree.** The tree with lab defaults (0.7347) is a deliberate contrast: it stops at 3–4 leaves and prunes on misclassification. Grown deeper and pruned on deviance, it gives 0.7669 against 0.7689.
- **The headline gain.** The XGBoost-over-logit gain is 0.026 in both languages.
