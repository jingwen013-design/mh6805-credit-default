\section{Data}

\paragraph{Source and forward-looking design.}
The data are the \emph{Default of Credit Card Clients} panel of Yeh and Lien [10], UCI dataset 350: 30,000 card holders of a Taiwanese bank. Every predictor is measured over April--September 2005 and the label is the October 2005 payment, so no feature is observed after the outcome it predicts. Twenty-three raw predictors and six engineered ones give 29 (Table~\ref{tab:a-variables}). Code, data and every result file are public at \href{https://github.com/jingwen013-design/mh6805-credit-default}{\texttt{github.com/jingwen013-design/mh6805-credit-default}}; \texttt{code/run\_all.sh} regenerates every number from the public download under seed 2607526, and \texttt{code/freeze.py -{}-check} verifies the frozen test scorings. There are 6,636 defaulters against 23,364 non-defaulters (22.1\%); removing 35 exact duplicates leaves 6,630 against 23,335 at the same rate. A ``never default'' rule is already 77.9\% accurate while catching nobody, so accuracy selects nothing here; average precision has no-skill level 0.221, the prevalence.

\paragraph{What happens before the split.}
Three things, none estimated from the data (\texttt{code/common.py}): a fixed-codebook recoding of the undocumented EDUCATION and MARRIAGE codes to ``others'', row-wise algebra that builds the six engineered features from a client's own columns, and the removal of 35 byte-identical rows (0.12\%); everything estimated --- scaling, one-hot levels, resampling, thresholds --- lives inside the \texttt{Pipeline} and is re-fitted in every fold (Table~\ref{tab:a-quality}). One imputation is self-inflicted: \texttt{PAY\_RATIO\_MEAN} is undefined for 936 of the 29,965 clients (3.12\%) whose six bills are all non-positive and is set to 0 --- a filler that is not neutral (in the training partition they default at 37.0\% against 21.7\%) and that merges dormant accounts with genuine non-payers.

\paragraph{Exploratory evidence.}
Arrears dominate, and capacity modulates them: in the training partition the default rate moves 12.9\%, 33.9\%, 67.6\% from revolving credit to one and two months late in September, and falls from 32\% in the lowest credit-limit quintile to 14\% in the highest (Table~\ref{tab:a-gradients}). Bill size alone is worthless --- the six bill amounts have univariate AUC 0.50--0.52 --- but the same bills against the limit are not: utilisation is U-shaped, 24.2\% default among clients with no positive balance (zero bill or credit), a trough of 14.4\% at 4--13\% drawn, and 28.1\% in the top decile (utilisation 0.98 and above). Demographic gaps are a few points (men 24.5\%, women 20.6\%) beside the swing of a single month's status, and the six bills are collinear ($r = 0.80$--$0.95$), so any linear coefficient placed on them will be unstable (Figures~\ref{fig:eda-default-rate-by-pay1}--\ref{fig:eda-univariate-strength}).
