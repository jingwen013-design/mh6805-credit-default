\section{Conclusion and limitations}

\paragraph{Answers.}
(1)~\emph{Complexity}: the three ensembles tie, 0.025--0.026 CV AUC above the logit (XGBoost on test: $+0.0250$ [$+0.0183$, $+0.0314$]); a third of the gap is status coding, most of the rest one-variable shape, interactions small (training CV). (2)~\emph{Operating point}: from 2:1 to 20:1 the threshold saving exceeds the model saving: the cut-off, not the model, is the lever. (3)~\emph{Imbalance}: in-fold weighting and under-sampling move the intercept, not the ranking, and SMOTE costs the forest 0.0055 CV AUC; resampling before splitting fakes a 0.914 test AUC. (4)~\emph{Inference}: recent status dominates, as default continues a delinquency process; demographics add 0.002 training-CV AUC at a fairness cost; constraining only the arrears direction is almost free.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(i)~\emph{Retrospective test use} (\S3): until 19 October test AUC chose the bootstrap's reference model and the four models in the figures, and nine Platt variants were scored on 19 October after a 6 October review had applied their maps to test scores (Platt was pre-committed on 30 September); the test set is a dated report card, not a fresh holdout. (ii)~\emph{Illustrative cost ratio} (\S6). (iii)~\emph{Vintage and drift}: one Taiwanese bank at the onset of a card-debt crisis; base rates move several-fold over a cycle (US card delinquency 6.77\% in 2009Q2, 1.53\% in 2021Q3 [2], an analogy), so reuse needs PDs re-based to the new default rate, a re-estimated cost ratio and out-of-time validation. (iv)~\emph{Design}: a cross-section and one split; the ensembles were not nested, so their internal order cannot be separated from selection optimism; some grids end where CV might still rise (Table~\ref{tab:a-tuningchecks}; clearly so only at the forest's largest leaf size, 20); single seeds for the MLP, forest and XGBoost. (v)~\emph{Attributes and causality}: fairness numbers are a training-OOF diagnostic, not a compliance assessment, and SHAP, PDPs and coefficients describe fitted models, not causes. \emph{Future work}: an out-of-time holdout, bureau and income data, measured losses, an additive scorecard and nested multi-seed ensembles.
